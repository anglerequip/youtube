# AnglerMind: brand brief for videos

Caesar (2026-11-26): this repo is now the video and marketing factory for **AnglerMind**
(https://anglermind.com). It used to make Counterphysical science Shorts; the pipeline (script,
voice, 3D render, QA, review, queue, publish) stays, the brand and the purpose change.
The rebrand plan and what is still to do is in `docs/REBRAND.md`.

## What AnglerMind is

A free fishing decision platform. It answers "what, where and when do I fish" with real data:

| Tool | What it does (say it this way in videos) |
|---|---|
| Ask AnglerMind (`/ask`) | Ask a fishing question in plain words; the site's own engines answer it |
| Plan My Trip (`/plan`) | Pick the fish, the water and the day; it hands you to the right tools |
| Omni Map (`/omni-map`) | Real measured depth maps, ranked spots, wind, radar and rain timing |
| Solunar (`/solunar`) | Scores the fishing day and the best hours from sun, moon and weather |
| Lure Finder (`/lure-finder`) | Recommends a real lure and the exact factory colour for the conditions |
| Gear Finder (`/gear`) | Rods, reels and lines that genuinely balance |
| 3D Preview (`/3d-preview`) | Build a setup and inspect it in 3D |
| Species guide (`/species`) | 289 species: size, season, waters, methods and bait |
| Waters (`/waters`) | Surveyed Swedish lakes, shown only where real data exists |

Source of truth: `anglerequip/anglermind` (its `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/marketing-plan.md`). Check a
feature there before a video mentions it; never advertise something the site doesn't do.

## How it earns, and what that means for videos

AnglerMind sells nothing and has no checkout. It earns from tagged Amazon affiliate links on the site.

- Videos send people to **the site**, never straight to Amazon. No affiliate links in video
  descriptions, comments or captions (platform norms and Amazon's terms).
- The marketing plan (0.4) proposes a per-channel Amazon tracking ID for video, `anglermind-yt-20`;
  the site sets it, not the videos. Links in descriptions use plain `anglermind.com/<tool>` so they stay readable.
- The site states its affiliate disclosure on pages with buy links; videos don't need to repeat it
  unless a video shows a specific product and its price. Then say "links on the site are affiliate links".

## Audience and promise

Anglers of all levels, heavy on spin and fly, strong in Sweden and the Nordics, English first.
Proposed promise in one line: **"Know what to tie on, where to cast and when to go, before you leave home."**

Proposed tagline for sign-offs (Caesar to confirm): **"AnglerMind: fish smarter."** (Topic lines may vary it, for example
"AnglerMind: the right colour, on real data.")

## Content pillars

1. **Tool in action** (the main ad format): a real angler problem, the tool solving it, the answer
   on screen. "Muddy water after rain, 6 am, pike: here's the colour." Built from real tool output.
2. **Fishing science** (what the fishing Shorts already do well): why fish see colour the way they
   do, how sound travels from a boat, line strength. Ends with the tool that uses that science.
3. **Character-led skits**: our own AnglerMind character (being designed in the art-style thread)
   meets a fishing problem and the site fixes it. Original character only.

Every video still follows the honesty rules: real, checkable facts and real tool output. No
fabricated catches, reviews, records or engagement. If a tool has no data for a case, the video
doesn't pretend it does.

## Look and voice

- Brand colours (from the site's `globals.css`): olive `#52652c`, cream `#faf7ea`, sand `#f2edd9`,
  rust highlight `#b6552a`, lake blue `#4c7680`, dark ink `#232b1c`. Label cards: cream card with
  olive or rust text. These replace the Counterphysical red for labels.
- Logo: `AnglerMind LOGO.png` and `public/logo-mark.svg` in the site repo. Wordmark "AnglerMind"
  (capital A and M), never "Anglermind" or "Angler Mind".
- Writing: plain, friendly, specific. No em dashes anywhere (same rule as the site repo).
- Call to action: one line, last beat or description: "Try it free at anglermind.com". Never
  "like and subscribe".
